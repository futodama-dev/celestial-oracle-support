Privacy Policy

futodama ("we", "us", or "the Operator"), the operator of "Celestial Oracle" (the "App"), establishes this Privacy Policy regarding the handling of information related to users of the App.

1. Operator

Operator: futodama
Contact: futodama0610@gmail.com

2. Information Handled by the App

(1) Oracle Card Usage Data

The App may handle the following information in order to provide its features:

  • History of cards drawn
  • Reflections (answers that the user chooses to enter in response to each day's question)
  • Cards added to Favorites
  • Information about encountered cards
  • Dates on which cards were drawn and other information necessary for managing history

This information is generally stored locally on the user's device.

In the iOS version, when iCloud is available, the App may use Apple Inc.'s CloudKit service to synchronize supported data, including draw history, reflections, Favorites, and encountered cards, with the user's iCloud environment.

We do not store this information, including the content of reflections, on servers independently operated by us. However, only when the transfer of data to the Home Screen version of the Web version (see 2.(7)) is used, temporary data encrypted on the user's device is stored on Cloudflare for up to one hour.

(2) Settings Information

The App may store notification settings and other settings required for use of the App locally on the user's device.

Some settings are not synchronized through CloudKit and may be deleted when the App is uninstalled.

(3) Notifications

If enabled by the user, the App may send notifications related to the user's daily oracle card.

Notification permissions and settings can be changed through the device settings and the App settings.

(4) In-App Purchases

The App may offer additional decks or other digital content through Apple's App Store and StoreKit.

Payment processing is handled by Apple. We do not directly obtain the user's credit card number or other payment information.

The App may use transaction or entitlement information provided by Apple to provide or restore purchased content.

(5) Information Not Required for Normal Use

The App does not require registration for an account operated by us.

For normal use of the App, we do not require users to provide their name, postal address, telephone number, or email address.

The App's core features do not require access to location information, the camera, or microphone.

However, if a user contacts us by email or another method, we may receive information voluntarily provided by the user, such as an email address and the content of the inquiry.

(6) Web Version

In addition to the iPhone app (the "iOS version"), the App is available as a web version that runs in a web browser (https://celestial.futodama.app/) (the "Web version").

In the Web version, card history, reflections, Favorites, encountered cards, and related data, as well as settings, are stored locally in the user's browser (for example, in IndexedDB). We do not store this information on servers independently operated by us (except for the temporary storage for the transfer of data to the Home Screen version described in 2.(7)).

To allow the Web version to be used offline, it uses a Service Worker and the browser's cache (Cache Storage) to store the files needed to display the App in the browser.

The Web version does not currently provide iCloud/CloudKit synchronization, in-app purchases through the App Store/StoreKit, or notifications. Data in the Web version is therefore not synchronized with data in the iOS version.

The Web version of the App itself does not use cookies, except for the temporary cookie used for the transfer of data to the Home Screen version described in 2.(7).

(7) Transferring Data to the Home Screen Version

On devices such as iPhone and iPad, when the Web version is added to the Home Screen, the browser and the Web version added to the Home Screen (the "Home Screen version") keep their data in separate storage. We therefore offer a feature that transfers card history, reflections, Favorites, encountered cards, and related data recorded in the browser to the Home Screen version.

Only when this transfer is performed, the data needed for the transfer is encrypted on the user's device and temporarily stored on Cloudflare, in encrypted form, for up to one hour. The stored data is deleted when the transfer is completed or when this period expires.

For this transfer, a temporary cookie containing the information needed to retrieve and decrypt the data is also used for up to one hour. This cookie is not used for any purpose other than the transfer, and is not used for advertising or for tracking behavior.

Only if the cookie is not carried over to the Home Screen version, the same data is retrieved using a temporary code (valid for up to one hour and usable only once) shown in the Settings of the original browser.

(8) Measuring Use of the Web Version

To understand use of the Web version statistically and to evaluate our promotional activities, when the following actions take place in the Web version, the type of action and the minimal information below are sent to a Cloudflare aggregation service used by us and counted.

  • When the Web version is opened: campaign identification parameters contained in the URL (identifiers for the source, medium, campaign, and content), the display language, and whether it was launched as an app added to the home screen
  • When a card is drawn: the identifier of the deck used
  • When a reflection is saved: only the fact that it was saved

This measurement does not send the content of reflections, information about drawn cards or history, names, email addresses, or IDs for continuously identifying users or devices, and it does not use cookies.

3. Purposes of Use

Information handled by the App is used for the following purposes:

  • Providing and maintaining App functionality
  • Saving draw history, reflections, Favorites, and encountered cards
  • Synchronizing and restoring supported data through iCloud
  • Providing notifications configured by the user
  • Providing and restoring purchased content
  • Storing data so that the Web version can be used offline
  • Transferring data from the Web version to the Home Screen version
  • Understanding usage and display performance of the Web version statistically
  • Evaluating promotional activities for the Web version
  • Responding to bugs and improving the App
  • Responding to inquiries

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services.

Apple iCloud / CloudKit

CloudKit may be used to synchronize supported data, including draw history, Favorites, and encountered cards, with the user's iCloud environment.

Information handled by Apple services is subject to Apple's applicable privacy policies and terms.

Apple App Store / StoreKit

Apple's App Store and StoreKit may be used for purchasing additional content, verifying purchase status, and restoring previously purchased content.

Payment information is handled in accordance with Apple's applicable terms and policies.

Cloudflare (Cloudflare Pages / Cloudflare Web Analytics / Cloudflare Workers)

The Web version is delivered using Cloudflare Pages, provided by Cloudflare, Inc. When the Web version is accessed, information necessary for communication (such as IP address, browser type, and access time) may be processed by Cloudflare.

To understand usage and display performance of the Web version statistically, we also use Cloudflare Web Analytics. Cloudflare Web Analytics may handle information such as pages viewed, referrers, country or region, device and browser type, and performance. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics in a configuration that does not use cookies.

The measurement described in 2.(8) uses Cloudflare Pages Functions and Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine.

The transfer of data to the Home Screen version described in 2.(7) uses Cloudflare Pages Functions and Cloudflare Workers (Durable Objects) to store the encrypted temporary data.

Information handled by Cloudflare is subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy and other applicable terms.

Except where required by law or otherwise justified, we do not provide personal information directly obtained by us to third parties without the user's consent.

5. Advertising

The App currently does not display third-party advertising.

Accordingly, the App does not independently use advertising identifiers or third-party advertising services for tracking purposes.

If advertising services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated as necessary.

6. Data Storage and Deletion

Data stored locally on the device may be deleted when the App is uninstalled.

However, supported data synchronized through CloudKit, including draw history, reflections, Favorites, and encountered cards, may be restored after reinstalling the App or changing devices when the same Apple Account and compatible iCloud environment are used.

Certain local settings, including notification settings, may not be restored after reinstalling the App.

Data in the Web version is stored in the user's browser. If the user deletes site data or browsing data in the browser, data in the Web version, including card history, reflections, and Favorites, may also be deleted, and we cannot restore it. Stored data may also be deleted depending on the browser's specifications or settings.

Encrypted temporary data stored for the transfer of data to the Home Screen version described in 2.(7) is deleted when the transfer is completed or, at the latest, one hour after it was stored.

Storage, synchronization, and deletion of data stored in iCloud are handled using Apple's iCloud and CloudKit systems.

We do not guarantee that all data will always be synchronized or restored successfully, as this may depend on network conditions, iCloud availability, Apple services, or other circumstances.

Information voluntarily provided through inquiries may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the inquiry or comply with legal obligations.
